Referrer agreement

Version 1.0 · Updated 14 November 2026

This agreement is between Hodos ("Hodos", "we") and the person introducing shops to the Hodos map ("you", "the referrer"). It is the only agreement that lets you earn from a referral, and it is sent to you privately for signature. Do not forward it, publish it, or share it.

Programme availability. The referrer programme is open in Nepal only at this time. Hodos decides availability server-side from the coordinates you submit and the payout wallet you register; a location alone does not qualify you.

Hodos will not ask you to copy, forward or screenshot this document. If anyone claiming to be Hodos asks you to do that, it is not us: stop and email __HODOS_CONTACT__.

1. Parties

Hodos — operated by Devilabs (__HODOS_CONTACT__).

The referrer — the name, contact and Nepali payout wallet completed in the signature block below.

2. What you are being asked to do

You introduce a shop to Hodos. If that shop buys a software placement on the live map, you earn a promotional referral commission. Hodos is a software service provider — not a bank, deposit-taking institution, wallet custodian or financial intermediary. Payments from shops are commercial software revenue belonging solely to Hodos, and your referral balance is an unsegregated promotional commission ledger that matures for payout after thirty (30) days. You are not employed by Hodos, you are not an agent of Hodos, and you have no authority to make any promise on our behalf.

3. What you earn

ItemTerm
Your share60% of every software placement payment the shop you introduced makes (initial placement and every renewal/extension). Hodos keeps 40%.
What it is calculated onThe amount Hodos actually records for the placement when the shop's payment reference is submitted. It is computed by our server from the placement record — never from a figure you or the shop submit.
When it is creditedImmediately shown in your referral account when the shop's placement payment is recorded and its pin is published, and unlocked for cashout thirty (30) days (1 month) after each payment.
One shop, one referrerA shop can be referred once, by one person, permanently. The longer the shop stays on the map and renews its pin, the more you earn.
PayoutsTo a Nepali wallet only — eSewa, Khalti or IME Pay — after the 30-day maturity window. The minimum cashout request is $0.10.
FeesHodos does not charge you a fee to be paid. Your wallet provider's own charges, if any, are yours.

4. What you must not do

  • Create a referral for a shop you do not have a real relationship with, or claim a shop that is already referred.
  • Impersonate a shop, or promise a shop anything about pricing, placement or coverage.
  • Submit false coordinates, or use a location you are not actually at, to qualify for the programme.
  • Register a payout wallet that is not yours, or one that is not a Nepali wallet.
  • Attempt to earn from your own shop, or from a shop you control, unless Hodos agrees to it in writing.
  • Copy, forward, publish or screenshot this agreement, or any confidential material you receive from Hodos.

5. Confidential information

5.1

Confidential Information means anything you learn from Hodos that is not public, including: this agreement; the terms Hodos offers a shop; the identity or earnings of any other referrer; our early feature plans; our internal tooling; and anything marked confidential.

5.2

Keep it to yourself, use it only to perform this agreement, and disclose it to no one. This survives the end of the agreement for three (3) years, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

5.3

You must not disclose a shop's data to another shop, and you will never receive driver data, journeys, routes or any personal information about anyone who drives past the shop. If you are offered such data, do not accept it and tell us.

6. Fraud, false claims and suspension

Hodos may refuse, reverse or reclaim any earning it determines was obtained by fraud, a false claim, a duplicate shop, a spoofed location or a payout account that is not yours. Where we can, we will tell you why. Reversals are final and are not a penalty: an earning that was never valid is simply removed.

7. Why we need a payout wallet

Payment to a Nepali wallet is how the programme stays open only where it is available. It also means we never need to store bank details. We keep your wallet number so we can pay you, and we show it masked back to you.

8. No employment, no exclusivity

This is not employment, a partnership, a franchise or an agency. It is non-exclusive: you may introduce the same shop to anyone else, and Hodos may work with any other referrer. Nothing here entitles you to a particular number of referrals or a particular income.

9. Term and termination

This agreement starts when both parties sign and continues while you have any referral or any unpaid balance. Either party may end it with written notice. Referrals already credited stay yours until paid; referrals that depend on a pin that has not been paid for earn nothing. Clauses 4, 5, 6, 10 and 11 survive.

10. Liability

Hodos provides the map as it is, and does not promise a number of shops, referrals or earnings. To the extent the law allows, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, and Hodos' total liability is limited to the amount you were paid under this agreement in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability for fraud or for anything that cannot be limited by law.

11. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Nepal, and the courts of Kathmandu have exclusive jurisdiction. The parties will first try to resolve a dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days of written notice.

12. Entire agreement

This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and replaces earlier discussions. A change must be in writing and signed by both parties.
