This agreement is between Hodos ("Hodos", "we") and the shop identified in the signature block ("Partner", "you"). It covers listing your shop on the Hodos live map, how we handle each other's confidential information, and how placements work. Please read it before you publish a pin.

This document is a working draft prepared for signing. Hodos will have it reviewed by a Nepali-qualified advocate before offering it more widely. If any term conflicts with mandatory law in Nepal or your jurisdiction, that law applies and the rest of the agreement stands.

2. What Hodos is

Hodos is a software platform and live map of road conditions built from the phones of drivers already on the road. Hodos is a seller of software services — not a bank, financial institution, escrow agent or wallet custodian. Any fees paid to Hodos are commercial revenue for software placement services and become Hodos' sole property upon receipt.

A Placement is one real local shop listing pin on the live map: your shop's name, one line of text, a category and a position. Hodos charges 0% commission on your sales and never publishes fake or synthetic listings.

3. Confidential information

3.1

Confidential Information means anything one party learns from the other that is not public, including: unpublished pricing and discount terms; commercial plans and roadmaps; internal processes and tooling; the identity and terms of your referrals and other partners; unpublished map features; support conversations; and any document marked or described as confidential.

3.2

Each party will use Confidential Information only to perform this agreement, will protect it with the same care it uses for its own confidential information (and never less than reasonable care), and will disclose it only to staff and contractors who need it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

3.3

These obligations do not apply to information that: is or becomes public through no breach of this agreement; was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure; is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence; is independently developed without using the other party's Confidential Information; or must be disclosed by law or a competent authority, provided the receiving party gives prompt notice where it lawfully can.

3.4

These obligations continue for three (3) years after this agreement ends. They do not expire for trade secrets, which remain protected for as long as they remain secret.

4. Road data

Road observations are anonymous and belong to the shared map. A Partner has no access to driver journeys, identities, routes, or any individual's data, and must never ask for it.

Hodos will not share a Partner's data with another Partner. Placement statistics surface only as anonymous totals.

5.1 Editorial limits

A placement gives your real shop visibility on the map. It does not buy an opinion, a rating, a hazard classification, a road-quality score, or any effect on the road data shown to drivers. Hodos will not alter road data for a Partner, and will not remove a hazard near a Partner at the Partner's request.

5.2 Suspension

Hodos may remove a pin before its expiry, without refund, where the content is unlawful, misleading, fake, or infringes another party's rights, or where the Partner breaches clause 3 or 4. Where practical, Hodos will tell you why first.

6. Referrals

After helping 289 people earn ₹2,00,000+ (2 lakh+), the Hodos referral programme is currently paused while shop pins are free initially. New referral codes are not being issued during the free-pin period, and anyone interested can join the waitlist at /referrals. Existing matured balances from prior paid placements remain governed by the referrer agreement.

7. Your responsibilities

  • You are entitled to list the shop, and the details you give are accurate.
  • You will honour offers you describe on your pin, to the standard you would give any customer.
  • You will keep your contact details current so we can reach you about your placement.
  • You will not use the placement to collect personal data from drivers.

8. Our responsibilities

  • We will publish a paid pin for the days purchased, and remove it when they expire.
  • We will not disclose your Confidential Information except as this agreement allows.
  • We will tell you about a material change to how placements work before it applies to a placement you have already bought.

9. Liability

Hodos provides the map as it is. We do not promise a number of views, visitors, or sales, and we do not control road conditions, traffic, or whether a driver stops at your shop.

To the extent the law allows, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Hodos' total liability under this agreement is limited to the amount of placement fees you paid in the twelve months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability for fraud, or for anything that cannot be limited by law.

10. Term and termination

This agreement starts when both parties sign and continues while you have placements or confidential information in scope. Either party may end it with written notice; placements already paid for run to their expiry. Clauses 3, 4, 9 and 11 survive termination.

11. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Nepal, and the courts of Kathmandu have exclusive jurisdiction. The parties will first attempt to resolve a dispute in good faith within thirty (30) days of written notice.

12. Entire agreement

This agreement, together with the price quoted at checkout for a specific placement, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject. It replaces earlier discussions. A change must be in writing and signed by both parties, except that Hodos may update the standard terms for placements bought after the update is published.
