Open Road Data for Cities & Communities

All road condition and defect observations collected through Hodos are public and open. City road departments, municipal engineers, and local community groups can directly export and use this data for road maintenance.

Free GeoJSON & GPX Exports: Load crowdsourced potholes and roughness directly into QGIS, ArcGIS, or civil repair work orders.
ASTM E1926 Standard: Roughness metrics modeled on the International Roughness Index (IRI m/km) to prioritize resurfacing.
Publicly Auditable: Transparent, open-source algorithms under the MIT License—no proprietary lock-in.
