Core Engineering

Built for unattended precision.

Existing tools require drivers to dangerously tap screens while driving. Hodos operates silently in the background with zero user interaction.

Adaptive MAD Filtering

Dynamic Median Absolute Deviation noise-floor tracking continuously self-calibrates to heavy bus suspensions, motorbikes, or cars without manual tuning.

Zero Distraction Guarantee

100% silent background foreground-service. The smartphone never chimes, never vibrates on bumps, and never displays intrusive popups while driving.

On-Device Privacy Perimeter

The first and last 250 meters of every trip are excised directly on the phone. Home and office locations never leave device RAM.

Spatial Corroboration

Multi-pass density clustering (DBSCAN) aggregates observations from multiple vehicles to confirm persistent physical damage over transient vehicle jolts.

Dual-License Sustainability

Software code is MIT licensed and fully auditable. The hazard dataset is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, preserving free access for citizens while licensing commercial fleets.

Open GIS Formats

One-click GeoJSON and GPX exports ready for immediate import into QGIS, Kepler.gl, ArcGIS, or municipal road maintenance dispatch systems.
