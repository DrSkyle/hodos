Good to know.

Clear answers on cost, sensors, privacy, and shop pins.

Does it cost anything for drivers?

No. The app, the public map, and your on-device road history are free. The Garage catalogue and shop placements fund the infrastructure.

Can I run Hodos alongside my navigation or ride app?

Yes. On Android, turn automatic mapping on once and it runs quietly in the background while you navigate or take rides. In the web app, keep the tab open while driving—browsers pause motion sensors when a tab is hidden.

Where does my data go?

Route traces stay on your phone. Only anonymous, quantised hazard observations are sent, with no account and no advertising ID. On Android, the first and last 250 metres of every journey are trimmed. Full details live in the privacy notice.

I run a shop in Nepal. Is placing a pin really free right now?

Yes. Shop pins in Nepal are completely free initially so local garages, tyre shops, cafés, and pharmacies can get on the map and welcome riders redeeming monthly perks. Elsewhere, daily rates start at $0.19 in local currency with no contract.

Does a shop placement ever alter hazard data or cover the road?

Never. A shop placement is a quiet pin alongside the road, never a pop-up over navigation, and road-hazard corroboration cannot be bought or edited.

How do AI assistants query Hodos road data?

AI agents follow AGENTS.md to provision a key and call the metered Appwrite endpoint for hazards, roads, partners, or garage—no personal user data is ever exposed.
