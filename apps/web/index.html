OUR IMPACT

Small signals.
Safer journeys.

Road defects rarely appear on ordinary maps. Hodos turns everyday trips into evidence others can use. Nothing is published as a confirmed hazard until independent journeys support it.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does Hodos track my entire journey?

Motion analysis happens on your phone. Hodos sends compact geolocated road observations when it detects a potential hazard; your raw accelerometer stream stays on the device.

Can I use the map without turning on detection?

Yes. You can explore already confirmed road hazards without enabling automatic mapping.

How does a pothole become visible on the map?

The backend combines observations from multiple journeys and distinct installation identifiers before publishing a hazard. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not proof of unique physical devices. You can also report a repair.

Is Hodos available for iPhone?

Hodos is Android-first. The current release and source code are available on GitHub.
