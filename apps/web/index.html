04 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does Hodos track my entire journey?

Motion analysis happens on your phone. Hodos submits compact geolocated road observations when mapping is enabled, not raw accelerometer data or your complete GPS route.

Can I use the map without turning on detection?

Yes. You can explore publicly confirmed road hazards without enabling automatic mapping.

When does a hazard appear on the map?

Hodos checks for repeated observations from different app installations before publishing a hazard. People can also report when a road has been repaired.

Can I save maps for offline use?

The Android app can save a nearby 20 km map area over Wi-Fi after you allow location. Saved tiles work offline; other regions and fresh hazard updates need internet.

Is Hodos available for iPhone?

Hodos is Android-first and in beta testing. iPhone support is not available yet.
