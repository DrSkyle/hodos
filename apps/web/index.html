FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Does Hodos track my entire journey?

Motion analysis happens on your phone. Hodos sends compact geolocated road observations when it detects a potential hazard; your raw accelerometer stream stays on the device.

Can I use the map without turning on detection?

Yes. You can explore already confirmed road hazards without enabling automatic mapping.

When does a hazard appear on the map?

Hodos checks for repeated observations from different app installations before showing a hazard. You can also report when one has been repaired.

Can Hodos download maps for offline driving?

On Android, Hodos can save a 20 km nearby area over Wi-Fi after you allow location. You can turn automatic downloads off, save a region manually, or remove maps in the Mapping tab. Previously saved map tiles work offline, but new hazard reports and other regions need internet.

Is Hodos available for iPhone?

Hodos is Android-first and still in beta testing. Check the latest Android release notes before installing; real-road sensor performance is still being verified.
