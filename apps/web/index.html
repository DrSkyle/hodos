Questions

Good to know.

Does Hodos track my entire route?

No. Motion analysis happens on your phone. Hodos only submits compact road observations when mapping is enabled—never raw sensor streams or your full GPS history.

Can I browse the map without turning on sensing?

Yes. Anyone can explore the live map on the web or in the Android app without enabling background mapping.

How are hazards color-coded on the live map?

Potholes are shown in crimson, rough road stretches in warm amber, speed bumps in violet, and repaired spots in emerald—keeping your blue location dot unmistakably clear.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Android app queues observations locally when you're offline and can automatically cache a 20 km nearby map area over Wi-Fi.
