Good to know.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app, the map and the data on your phone are free. The Garage and shop placements pay for it.

Can I run Hodos alongside my navigation or ride app?

Yes. Turn automatic mapping on once and it runs quietly in the background while you navigate, accept rides or follow directions in whatever app you use.

Where does my data go?

Route traces stay on your phone. Only anonymous hazard reports are published, and the first and last 250 metres of every journey are trimmed before anything leaves the device.

I run a shop. How much is a pin?

From $0.19 a day for much of the world, $0.79 for middle-income markets and $1.99 where advertising costs more. You pay in your own currency, there is no contract, and the pin comes down when the days run out.

Does a placement change the map?

Never. It is a pin next to the road, not an ad over it, and hazard data is never for sale.
