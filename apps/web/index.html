Good to know.

Short answers about the Garage, how Hodos rides along, and what it never collects.

Does buying from the Garage cost more?

No. You pay the retailer's price, with their returns and warranty. Buying through a shelf link is how the free map gets funded.

Why these eight categories?

They are the parts that actually fail on poor roads — a tire that loses pressure, a mount that lets go, wipers that smear. Anything that cannot be linked to a real, in-stock offer is not shown.

Can I run Hodos alongside my navigation or ride app?

Yes. Turn automatic mapping on once and Hodos runs silently in the background while you navigate, accept rides, or follow directions in whatever app you already use.

How does Hodos make money if the app is free?

Three ways: the Garage, licensing of aggregated road-quality data, and partner placements. None of them change what the map shows.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Android app queues observations locally when you're offline and can automatically cache a 20 km nearby map area over Wi-Fi. The Garage needs a connection because it reads live shop prices.
