How it works

From a single jolt in a pocket to a verified pin on the map.

01 / 03
01 · SENSE ON DEVICE

A phone in a pocket feels the impact

While you drive, the on-device detector separates sharp vertical road impacts from engine vibration and logs the coordinates locally. A single jolt never reaches the public map alone.

02 · CORROBORATE BY TRIP

Independent vehicles confirm the same spot

When multiple distinct devices and trips record an impact within the same 15-metre cell, Hodos confirms the defect is in the road—not a dropped phone or random bump.

03 · PUBLISH & HEAL

The pin goes live, and retires once fixed

Verified hazards appear on the public map and API. Later, when three vehicles pass over that spot smoothly without an impact, the hazard marks itself repaired.

REFERENCE NOTES · FAQ & PRIVACY

Good to know.

Does it cost anything for drivers?

No. The app, the public map, and your on-device road history are free. The Garage catalogue and shop placements fund the infrastructure.

Can I run Hodos alongside my navigation or ride app?

Yes. On Android, turn automatic mapping on once and it runs quietly in the background while you navigate or take rides. In the web app (/app), keep the tab open while driving—browsers pause motion sensors when a tab is hidden or the screen locks.

Where does my data go?

Route traces stay on your phone. Only anonymous, quantised hazard observations are sent, with no account and no advertising ID. On Android, the first and last 250 metres of every journey are trimmed before anything leaves the device. Full details live in the privacy notice.

I run a shop in Nepal. Is placing a pin really free right now?

Yes. Shop pins in Nepal are completely free initially so local garages, tyre shops, cafés, and pharmacies can get on the map and welcome riders redeeming monthly perks. Elsewhere, daily rates start at $0.19 in local currency with no contract.

Does a shop placement ever alter hazard data or cover the road?

Never. A shop placement is a quiet pin alongside the road, never a pop-up over navigation, and road-hazard corroboration cannot be bought or edited.
