HOW IT WORKS / 001

Just go where
you were going.

Maps usually need someone to report a problem. Hodos learns from the movement of phones already travelling those roads.

01 /
↝

Set it once

Enable automatic mapping in the Android app, then carry on with your normal journeys.

02 /
⌁

Feel the road

On-device motion analysis finds likely potholes, rough patches and speed bumps without recording raw traces online.

03 /
◎

Trust the evidence

Independent journeys corroborate hazards. People can also report that a road was repaired.

WHAT MATTERS / 002

Useful in public.
Careful in private.

01

Raw motion data stays on the phone.

Hodos submits compact road observations, not continuous sensor recordings.

02

One report isn't the whole story.

The shared map uses independent evidence and records repair confirmations.

03

The project is open source.

Read the code, inspect the decisions, and help improve how your roads are represented.
