Questions

Good to know.

Can I run Hodos alongside my navigation or ride app?

Yes. Turn automatic mapping on once and Hodos runs silently in the background while you navigate, accept rides, or follow directions in whatever app you already use.

How does Hodos make money if the app is free?

Two ways, both visible: commercial licensing of aggregated road-quality data to fleets, insurers, and cities, and clearly badged offers from partner shops inside the app and map.

How are hazards color-coded on the live map?

Potholes are shown in crimson, rough road stretches in warm amber, speed bumps in violet, and repaired spots in emerald—keeping your blue location dot unmistakably clear.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Android app queues observations locally when you're offline and can automatically cache a 20 km nearby map area over Wi-Fi.
