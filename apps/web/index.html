01

Dynamic Noise Floor Tracking

Continuous Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) envelope tracking calculates the chassis vibration baseline in real-time. Heavy bus leaf springs or motorcycle engine vibrations dynamically raise the baseline so normal vehicle rumble is ignored.

02

Zero Driver Distraction

Completely silent background execution. Android Activity Recognition arms sampling only during vehicular movement. No popups, no buttons to tap while driving, and zero haptic vibrations when striking bumps.

03

Privacy by Construction

Raw motion traces stay strictly in device RAM. The first and last 250 meters of every trip are trimmed on-device so residential streets and endpoints are never transmitted. Hazards only publish after multi-trip independent corroboration.

TECHNICAL QUESTIONS

Answers regarding global sensor filtering, device mounting, battery usage, and dataset governance.

Does Hodos vibrate my phone when hitting a pothole?

No. Haptic vibration is strictly disabled. The app runs completely silently in your pocket or vehicle mount without interrupting you or distracting drivers.

How does Hodos prevent false alarms from vehicle vibration?

Continuous Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) noise-floor tracking dynamically adjusts sensitivity. Continuous chassis vibration from heavy trucks, buses, or motorcycles automatically raises the detection threshold so normal engine rumble is ignored.

Can commercial logistics fleets extract this data for free?

No. The crowdsourced dataset is protected under the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. While citizens, civic tech groups, and universities can access it freely for non-commercial research, commercial entities (logistics fleets, ride-hailing services, insurance underwriters) must obtain an enterprise commercial license from Devilabs.

Does Hodos work in any country?

Yes. The sensor signal algorithms and GPS observation pipeline operate autonomously anywhere on Earth. OpenFreeMap vector basemaps are downloaded and cached automatically over Wi-Fi so the app operates even across remote offline corridors.
