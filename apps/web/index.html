04 / QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How does Hodos detect road hazards automatically?

Hodos runs a lightweight background service that evaluates continuous accelerometer and gyroscope data with Median Absolute Deviation (MAD) envelope tracking. When you pass over a pothole or severe bump, the sudden impulse is recorded and verified without manual input.

How does Hodos prevent false alarms from vehicle vibrations?

Continuous noise-floor tracking dynamically adjusts sensitivity. Continuous chassis vibration from buses, trucks, or motorcycles automatically raises the baseline filter so only true road surface shocks trigger an observation.

Who has access to the road condition data?

Everyone. The road defect map and aggregated condition layers are public and open under the MIT License. Municipal road authorities, civil engineers, and citizens can freely view live hazards or download GeoJSON and GPX files for GIS mapping and repair planning.

Does Hodos vibrate my phone when hitting a bump?

No. Haptic vibration is disabled so your phone stays quiet in your pocket or vehicle mount without distracting you.
