# Surface: Hodos Web & Live Defect Map

- **Scope:** Full web platform (`apps/web` landing showcase + `apps/web/map` live defect explorer).
- **Mode:** Persuade (showcase & landing) / Operate (live map explorer).
- **Audience:** Everyday commuters and riders in Nepal + civic authorities and logistics dispatchers.
- **Durable Constraints:** Zero emojis, zero intrusive haptics, strictly real corroborated backend data, authentic v1.1.20 master brandmark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The road is an unyielding physical reality, not abstract cloud software. Refuses generic SaaS gradient hero headers, floating translucent glass cards, and artificial demo lines. Anchors defect detection to physical highway milestones, chiseled distance posts, and municipal accountability.

OWN-WORLD: Ground of weathered concrete #F4F3EE and deep asphalt #121518. Accented with highway caution chrome yellow #F5B800 and high-visibility survey red #D9381E. Components borrow physical milestone silhouettes (arched stone caps, chiseled border bevels, stamped route badges like H-01 / KM 438), heavy industrial display grotesque, and tabular monospaced survey coordinates.

STORY: The visitor instantly understands that Hodos passively maps real road hazards without human taps or distraction, sees the live milestone of 438 verified hazards across Nepal, tests the interactive motion accelerometer simulator, and moves directly to the live defect map or APK download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Industrial highway milestone masthead with v1.1.20 master mark, live verified hazard milestone badge (438 HAZARDS CATALOGED), high-contrast dual-action buttons (Explore Live Map in highway chrome yellow, Download v1.2.0 APK in concrete stone outline), and an active road roughness cross-section monitor.

FORM: Candidate 1 (Highway Milestone & Survey Monolith), selected as Impeccable's Pick from the 7 grounded candidates. Seed reference: seed:811a1ce9e84c2fcc.
