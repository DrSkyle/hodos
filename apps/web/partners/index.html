Why list on Hodos (and how your pin actually works)

Initial shop pins are completely free everywhere — no setup fee, no account, no ad auction, and 0% commission on anything you sell.

  • Direct to AI assistants — not just Google search

    Every live pin is served directly through the Hodos API and attached to road-hazard queries for your area. When a driver asks an AI assistant for a nearby tyre shop, garage, charger, or food stop, your real listing is returned — with zero pay-to-rank ad auctions.

  • How your pin appears on the map (transparent rules)

    Your pin goes live immediately on the map, in the Garage directory, and in the API. In quiet areas every shop shows directly; in busy streets with more than 12 shops in one view, nearby pins cluster cleanly and prioritize roadside essentials & rider perks so road hazards stay readable.

  • Your line, in your words

    One line of text on the pin — what you fix, serve, or offer, plus opening hours. No fake listings and no third-party reviews hijacking your card.

  • Rider perks & post-trip nudges

    After rough trips (never while moving), the app quietly surfaces nearby tyre, alignment, and service shops offering rider checks, and draws monthly perk redemption lists from live pins.

  • Anonymous monthly pass counts

    Save your Pin Reference ID after submitting to see how many distinct devices physically drove past your shop this month — zero driver identities exposed.

  • Zero popups over navigation

    Your pin sits cleanly on the map with a direct Directions button. It never interrupts driving and never alters road-hazard data.
