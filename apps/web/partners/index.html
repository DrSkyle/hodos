What a placement includes

Everything below is included in the daily price. No setup fee, no account, no contract, no commission on anything you sell.

  • A pin on the live map

    Your shop sits on the same map riders already open to avoid potholes, so it is seen at the moment they are thinking about their vehicle.

  • Your line, in your words

    One line of text on the pin — a repair, a price, opening hours. Edited by us whenever you ask, at no extra cost.

  • Visible to AI agents

    Every live pin is served by the Hodos API, so when someone asks their own AI assistant "where can I fix a puncture near me", your shop is one of the shops the assistant can find, name and recommend. The same listing works for people browsing and for assistants answering.

  • Rider perks that point at you

    Riders earn a free tire check, chain lube or alignment check by distance driven each month. Their app names the partner shops they can redeem at — and that list is drawn from live pins, so joining puts you in it.

  • Real pass numbers

    Anonymous: we count how many distinct devices passed your shop this month, never who they were. You can see whether the placement is doing anything.

  • Never over the road ahead

    A placement never covers the route, never interrupts navigation and never changes hazard data. It is a pin, not an ad.
