What a placement includes

Everything below is included for free initially. No setup fee, no app account, no contract, and 0% commission on anything you sell.

  • A pin on the live road map

    Your shop sits on the same map riders open to avoid potholes and rough roads — seen at the exact moment they are on the road.

  • Your offer, in your words

    One line of text on the pin — a service, a rate, opening hours. Updated whenever you ask, at no extra cost.

  • Visible to AI assistants

    Every live pin is served by the Hodos API. When someone asks their AI assistant "where can I fix a puncture near me", active pins are what it finds and recommends.

  • Rider perks that point at you

    Riders earn a free tyre check, chain lube, or alignment check each month and redeem them at active partner shops.

  • Real pass numbers

    Anonymous count of how many distinct rider devices passed your shop this month, so you can see the real traffic.

  • Pay via UPI / IMPS, zero lock-in

    Start with 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days. Renew only if it works for your shop.
