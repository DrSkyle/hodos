# Ownership lives on the device, not in an account

A user's sense of the map belonging to them — their area, their trips, their
tally — is stored locally and never uploaded as an identity. Adding accounts
would have made the app feel personal, but it would also have broken the thing
that makes it worth using: you contribute by driving past, not by signing up.

_Consequences:_ reinstalling clears the personal view. The tally is honest about
this rather than pretending to be an account. The same choice keeps the privacy
story simple: there is no server-side profile to leak or delete.
